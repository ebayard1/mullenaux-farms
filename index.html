Pastured Poultry

At Mullenaux Farms the layer chicken is our staple livestock animal

Easily managed

Chickens are easily contained, moved, fed, watered and replaced, due to their natural instinct to stay near their coops and flocks, their great range of eating habits, and small size.

Consistently Producing

Most livestock have a breeding season, a growing season, and a harvesting season, all for one payday. Layer chicken flocks however produce a sellable product every day of the year, making for a much steadier business model

High Demand

Americans on average eat nearly 300 eggs a year. Our pasture-raised, no-corn, no-soy eggs beat most store-bought eggs on both ethics and nutrition. Farm stores in the Phoenix area sell similar products for over 10 dollars a dozen, and they are looking for more producers.

Agroforestry

Agroforestry is the use of trees to support livestock in providing shade, habitat, and feed

Here in the valley trees provide an extreme advantage in pasture raising animals, as they build soil nutrients and hydration, decompacting the ground allowing forage to grow beneath them
